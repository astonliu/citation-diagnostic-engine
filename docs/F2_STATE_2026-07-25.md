## Closed decisions / adjudications

- **Seed-37 precision is 33.3%, not 90%.** The 5× HIGH rate versus seed 7 is
  over-flagging, not more F2. Seed 7's 90% came from n=10.
- **Page ranges: PubMed elides end pages, citations write them out.** 7,131 of
  23,370 rows (30.5%) read as page disagreement when the pages are identical. This
  is the single largest defect found. Everything downstream consumed a corrupted field.
- **Physical-location identity is the right F2 trigger, inverted.** Same pages +
  volume + journal means the identifier points at the correct physical article; two
  articles cannot share that slot. Full agreement holds on 18,349 of 23,370 rows
  (78.5%), so agreement is the norm and its *absence* is the signal. Rationale: ZD's
  argument that A is unfindable and B always stands in its place.
- **`10.1101/` is not a preprint-only registrant.** Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory
  Press shares it with Genome Research, Genes & Development, Learning & Memory and
  the CSH Perspectives series. 79 seed-37 rows misclassified against 14 real preprints.
- **Title furniture is a parser defect, not evidence of non-F2.** Verdict is "not yet
  judgeable", with two exits: re-parse and re-score (obscured handle, our bug), or
  unscoreable (absent handle, source bug). Excising leading furniture drives four rows
  to `title_sim = 1.0000`.
- **Trailing-title excision rejected.** 4,086 rows end with a capitalized proper noun
  not in the author list, against 2 that do.
- **`written_authors` cannot anchor excision, and is itself corrupt on these rows.**
  Names were misallocated by the splitter, not duplicated. This is why adding
  `first_author_match` to the physical-location conjunction *reduces* its effect.
- **Hyphen normalization rejected.** Useful alone, pure TP loss once the other rules
  are in place.
- **Whitespace collapsing: dead end, tested.** Resolved titles carry doubled internal
  spaces and this looked like a corpus-wide similarity depressant. `normalize_title`
  already collapses whitespace. Do not revisit.
- **`is_preprint_source` is claimed-side only.** It never asks whether the *resolved*
  record is a preprint, so the inverse case — ordinary-looking citation, claimed PMID
  resolving to a preprint — is undetectable. 79 seed-37 rows carry a preprint-prefixed
  DOI on the resolved side. Specced as a scoped addition to F2-B.
- **No searchable handle is decidable offline, and predicts non-F2.** Of the 51 HIGH
  rows, 7 have no usable handle (4 leading furniture, 3 bare container names) and all
  7 are non-F2, 0 TRUE_F2. Verdict is *not yet judgeable*, not non-F2: obscured handles
  get re-parsed and re-scored, absent handles go unscoreable. A mangled title and a
  wrong identifier are independent faults.
- **Errors occur on B's side too.** `PMC10227918:r138` recovers only to 0.8909 because
  PubMed's own title reads `CI-ATPase` for the citation's `Cl−-ATPase`. The resolved
  record is not automatically the clean side.
- **`journal_equivalent` rewrite deferred.** Containment-based and wrong in both
  directions, 1,216 rows rely on containment alone, but no audited row is known to be
  harmed. Token-alignment rewrite tested and rejected; a correct fix needs an NLM
  authority list. ZD's call: do not build it on inference from a mechanism.
- **Overfitting posture.** ZD's position, recorded: these are common, mechanism-level
  mistakes (page elision, registrant collision, bibliography formatting) that recur in
  any corpus, not patterns fitted to 51 rows. Accepted for the individual fixes. The
  *aggregate* precision figure stays labelled in-sample; the fresh seed produces the
  reportable number.
- **627 vs 700 source papers resolved.** The 73 drop between the selection file and
  frame construction; frame → freeze is lossless. All 73 have non-empty XML on Drive,
  so the frame builder extracted zero PMID-bearing references from them.
  `n_source_pmcids: 700` in both manifests does not describe the artifact, which has 627.
